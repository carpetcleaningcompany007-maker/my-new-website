A complete professional deep clean

We inspect and pre-vacuum first, then apply the correct targeted pre-spray, including an enzyme treatment where required. Next comes one of the most important stages: thorough agitation with a counter-rotating brush or rotary machine to break the dirt particles away from the carpet fibres. Only then do we extract using high-pressure hot water heated up to 220°F (professional steam cleaning), flushing the released soil from the carpet.

Helpful answers

Before you book

How long will my carpets take to dry?

Drying depends on fibre, airflow, temperature and the level of cleaning required. We give practical ventilation advice during your visit.

Can you help with pet accidents and odours?

Yes. Tell us what happened and where. We inspect the affected areas and explain what improvement is realistic before treatment.

Do I need to move all the furniture?

No. We agree what can sensibly be moved and what should remain in place. Small items are best cleared before arrival.

Which parts of Shrewsbury do you cover?

We cover Shrewsbury and surrounding SY postcode areas. Send your postcode and we will confirm availability.
